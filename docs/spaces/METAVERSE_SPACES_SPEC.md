# 🪐 Metaverse Spaces & World Zones Specification (space-station)

Technical specification for personal compartments, community hubs, access control policies, spatial object placement, and LIFE digital twin pilot zones in **MyZubster Space Station** (resolves Issue #33).

---

## 📌 Architecture
- **6 Space Archetypes**: `PERSONAL_HOME`, `ENTITY_SANCTUARY`, `COMMUNITY_HUB`, `EVENT_ARENA`, `MARKETPLACE_ZONE`, and `LIFE_PILOT_ZONE`.
- **4-Tier Access Control**: `PUBLIC`, `MEMBERS_ONLY`, `INVITE_ONLY`, and `PRIVATE_OWNER`.
- **Spatial Object Placement**: Allows authorized architects to mount interactive IoT monitors, telemetry boards, and cosmetic props.
- **Audited LIFE Pilot World**: Ships with `ZONE_ORTO_ROMA_PILOT` strictly tagged `isDemoData: true`.

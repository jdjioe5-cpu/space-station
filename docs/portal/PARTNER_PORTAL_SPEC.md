# 📊 Partner Portal & LIFE Reporting Exports Specification (space-station)

Technical specification for the Partner Operations Portal, LIFE environmental audit metrics, and multi-format structured data exports in **MyZubster Space Station** (resolves Issue #48).

---

## 📌 Features
- **Partner Access Control**: Role-gated portal views for academic, utility, and institutional leaders.
- **Automated LIFE Impact Synthesizer**: Tracks baseline vs. outcome CO2 reductions and verified hardware ratios.
- **Multi-Format Export Engine**:
  - Structured CSV for legacy GIS and spreadsheet analysis.
  - PDF-Ready JSON-LD schema for automated certification generation.
  - Cryptographic provenance signature (SHA-256) embedded in all exports.
